This codelist was developed to identify prescriptions for glyceryl trinitrate (GTN) in CPRD Aurum for use as a proxy indicator of symptomatic angina. Product codes were identified through term-based searches of the CPRD Aurum product browser using relevant glyceryl trinitrate product names and formulations. Retrieved codes were reviewed and retained where they represented GTN-containing products prescribed for the relief or management of angina symptoms. The codelist was created to support the identification of individuals with potential angina in the year prior to treatment assignment.

This ethnicity codelist was created using the validated CPRD ethnicity code list curated by the LSHTM / Exeter Diabetes team:

https://github.com/Exeter-Diabetes/CPRD-Codelists/blob/main/Medcodes/Sociodemographic%20and%20lifestyle/medcodelist_ethnicity_lshtm_updated24.txt

The source codelist includes primary‑care ethnicity codes recorded in UK electronic health records and mapped to standard ethnicity groupings.
Ethnicity codes were extracted and grouped to correspond to QRISK ethnicity categories

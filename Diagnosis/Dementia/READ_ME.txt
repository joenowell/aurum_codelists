This dementia diagnosis codelist was created using the following sources:
The codelist was initially adapted from the Exeter‑Diabetes Group CPRD codelists, available at:
https://github.com/Exeter-Diabetes/CPRD-Codelists/
An existing dementia codelist used within our research group was then incorporated to ensure full coverage of codes appearing in our datasets.
The combined list was manually reviewed and refined.
Only codes representing a clinical diagnosis of dementia were included.
Codes relating to delirium, amnestic syndromes, behavioural symptoms, or non‑dementia organic psychoses were excluded.
The final codelist reflects an all‑cause dementia phenotype

This codelist was created to identify severe liver disease for use as a baseline exclusion criterion (e.g. prior to statin initiation) in UK primary care electronic health records.
The codelist is based on SNOMED CT concepts from the OpenCodelists / OpenSAFELY severe liver disease codelist:

OpenSAFELY Severe Liver Disease (SNOMED)
https://www.opencodelists.org/codelist/opensafely/severe-liver-disease/5a033839

For use in CPRD Aurum, the codelist was restricted to diagnosis‑level records only (EmisCodeCategoryId = 32)

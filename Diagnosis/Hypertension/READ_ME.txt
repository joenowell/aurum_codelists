This hypertension diagnosis codelist was created using the phenotype:
Evangelos Kontopantelis, Ivan Olier, Claire Planner, David Reeves, Darren M Ashcroft, Linda Gask, Tim Doran, Siobhan Reilly.
PH533 / 1066 – Hypertension. Phenotype Library [Online]. 06 October 2021.
Available from: http://phenotypes.healthdatagateway.org/phenotypes/PH533/version/1066/detail/.
Accessed 09 April 2026.
The original phenotype was reviewed and used as the foundation for this codelist.
Additional hypertension diagnosis codes were identified and checked using the Exeter Diabetes Group CPRD codelists available at:
https://github.com/Exeter-Diabetes/CPRD-Codelists/
Only diagnosis codes for systemic hypertension were included.

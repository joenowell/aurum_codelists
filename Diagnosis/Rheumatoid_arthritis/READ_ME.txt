This codelist defines Rheumatoid arthritis (RA) following the definition used in QRISK3:
Rheumatoid arthritis
(diagnosis of rheumatoid arthritis, Felty’s syndrome, Caplan’s syndrome, adult onset Still’s disease, or inflammatory polyarthropathy not otherwise specified)
The codelist was developed using the HDR UK phenotype:
Evangelos Kontopantelis, Ivan Olier, Claire Planner, David Reeves, Darren M Ashcroft, Linda Gask, Tim Doran, Sioban Reilly.
PH555 / 1110 – Rheumatoid Arthritis. Phenotype Library [Online]. 06 October 2021.
Available from: http://phenotypes.healthdatagateway.org/phenotypes/PH555/version/1110/detail/.
Accessed 13 April 2026.
The HDR UK RA phenotype was reviewed and expanded by inspecting diagnosis codes from the Exeter Diabetes CPRD codelist repository. Only codes corresponding to the QRISK3 definition were retained:

Rheumatoid arthritis (all subtypes)
Felty’s syndrome
Caplan’s syndrome
Adult‑onset Still’s disease
Inflammatory polyarthropathy, not otherwise specified
Historical synonymous terms (e.g., rheumatic gout) used in UK clinical coding

To ensure reliability, only EMIS Code Category 32 (diagnosis) entries were included.

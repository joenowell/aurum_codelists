This codelist defines Severe Mental Illness (SMI) following the definition used in QRISK:
Severe mental illness
(this includes schizophrenia, bipolar disorder and moderate/severe depression)
 
The codelist was developed using the HDR UK phenotype:
Siobhan Reilly, Ivan Olier, Claire Planner, Tim Doran, David Reeves, Darren M Ashcroft, Linda Gask, Evangelos Kontopantelis.
PH638 / 1276 – Serious Mental Illness (SMI). Phenotype Library [Online]. 06 October 2021.
Available from: http://phenotypes.healthdatagateway.org/phenotypes/PH638/version/1276/detail/
Accessed 09 April 2026. 
The HDR UK SMI phenotype was reviewed and expanded by inspecting diagnosis codes from the Exeter Diabetes CPRD codelist repository. Only codes corresponding to:

Schizophrenia spectrum disorders
Bipolar affective disorders
Psychotic disorders
Moderate and severe depressive disorders (as used in QRISK3) were retained.


To ensure reliability, only EMIS Code Category 32 (diagnosis) entries were included.

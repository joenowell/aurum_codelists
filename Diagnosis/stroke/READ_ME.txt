This codelist was created to identify non‑fatal stroke events for use in a standard 3‑point Major Adverse Cardiovascular Event (MACE) outcome (myocardial infarction, stroke, cardiovascular death).
The starting point for the codelist was three published UK stroke phenotypes from the HDR UK Phenotype Library, which include mapped SNOMED CT and Read codes used in UK primary care:

These phenotypes were designed to capture stroke diagnoses recorded in UK electronic health records and provided the base set of stroke‑related codes.

Refinement for MACE use
The combined code lists were then reviewed and refined using Copilot guidance to ensure they were appropriate for a 3‑point MACE outcome in a time‑to‑event analysis.
Edits focused on keeping codes that clearly represent acute stroke events, including:

Ischaemic stroke
Haemorrhagic stroke
Cerebral infarction
Embolic and thrombotic stroke
Lacunar and territorial infarctions
Intracerebral, cerebellar, brainstem, and other intracranial haemorrhages
Stroke recorded as “unspecified” or “of uncertain pathology”

Codes were removed where they represented transient events, chronic cerebrovascular conditions, neurological syndromes, or vascular insufficiency without a stroke event, to avoid misclassification.

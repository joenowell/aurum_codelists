This codelist defines Type 1 diabetes mellitus and Type 2 diabetes mellitus using diagnosis codes only, following established HDR UK phenotypes and research definitions.
The Type 1 diabetes codelist was developed based on the HDR UK phenotype:
Alison K Wright, Evangelos Kontopantelis, Richard Emsley, Iain Buchan, Naveed Sattar, Martin K Rutter, Darren M. Ashcroft.
PH717 / 1434 – Type 1 Diabetes. Phenotype Library [Online]. 06 October 2021.
Available from: http://phenotypes.healthdatagateway.org/phenotypes/PH717/version/1434/detail/.
Accessed 14 April 2026.

The Type 2 diabetes codelist was developed based on the HDR UK phenotype:
Alison K Wright, Evangelos Kontopantelis, Richard Emsley, Iain Buchan, Naveed Sattar, Martin K Rutter, Darren M. Ashcroft. PH718 / 1436 - Type 2 Diabetes. Phenotype Library [Online]. 06 October 2021. Available from: http://phenotypes.healthdatagateway.org/phenotypes/PH718/version/1436/detail/. [Accessed 14 April 2026]

Only diagnosis codes explicitly indicating Type 1 or Type 2 diabetes were included.
Non–type-specific diabetes codes (e.g. “diabetes mellitus” or “diabetes NOS”), gestational diabetes, secondary diabetes, and administrative or screening codes were excluded to ensure high diagnostic specificity.

Only EMIS Code Category 32 (diagnosis) entries were retained.

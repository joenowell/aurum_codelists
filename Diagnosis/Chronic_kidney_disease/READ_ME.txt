This codelist defines Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) following the definition used in QRISK3:
Chronic kidney disease
(to include general practitioner–recorded diagnosis of CKD stage 3 as well as stages 4 and 5, and major chronic renal disease)
The codelist was developed using the HDR UK phenotype:
Evangelos Kontopantelis, Ivan Olier, Claire Planner, David Reeves, Darren M Ashcroft, Linda Gask, Tim Doran, Sioban Reilly.
PH510 / 1020 – Chronic Kidney Disease. Phenotype Library [Online]. 06 October 2021.
Available from: http://phenotypes.healthdatagateway.org/phenotypes/PH510/version/1020/detail/.
Accessed 13 April 2026.
The HDR UK CKD phenotype was reviewed and expanded using the NHS Primary Care SNOMED refset CKD_COD, and by inspecting diagnosis codes identified through structured SNOMED searches.
Only codes corresponding to the QRISK3 definition were retained:

CKD stage 3
CKD stage 4
CKD stage 5, including dialysis and transplant variants
Major chronic renal disease, including:

chronic renal failure / end‑stage renal disease
chronic glomerulonephritis
nephrotic syndromes
chronic pyelonephritis
renal transplant failure or history
diabetic, hypertensive, and drug‑induced CKD subtypes
other severe chronic renal diseases recognised in national SNOMED refsets



To ensure reliability, only EMIS Code Category 32 (diagnosis) entries were included.

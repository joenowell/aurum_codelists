This codelist defines Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE) for use in CPRD Aurum.
The definition follows the QRISK convention for lupus:
Systemic lupus erythematosus (including diagnosis of SLE, disseminated lupus erythematosus, or Libman–Sacks disease).
This codelist was created using:

A previous SLE codelist developed by our lab, based on CPRD Aurum diagnosis codes.
A *Read code search for all codes beginning with "N000*", which correspond to systemic lupus erythematosus and its systemic manifestations.
A term search for “Libman‑Sacks”, to ensure inclusion of cardiac manifestations of lupus recognised in QRISK definitions.
Manual review of all returned codes to confirm that each corresponds to a clinical diagnosis of lupus or a recognised systemic manifestation of SLE.

Only EMIS Code Category 32 (diagnosis) codes were included.

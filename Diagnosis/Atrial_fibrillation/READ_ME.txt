Both phenotypes were reviewed and combined to create a comprehensive list covering atrial fibrillation, atrial flutter, paroxysmal AF, persistent AF, permanent AF, chronic AF, and related clinically valid subtypes.
All codes were checked, and only EMIS Category 32 (diagnosis) codes were included.
Codes from other EMIS categories (e.g., administrative or encounter codes) were excluded.
This codelist was further cross‑checked against a previous AF codelist developed within our lab

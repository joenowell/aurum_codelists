This COPD diagnosis codelist is based on the list originally published in:
Evangelos Kontopantelis, Ivan Olier, Claire Panner, David Reeves, Darren M Ashcroft, Linda Gask, Tim Doran, Siobhan Reilly. "Primary care consultation rates among people with and without severe mental illness: a UK cohort study using the Clinical Practice Research Datalink." BMJ Open 2015; 5:e008650.
The original version is available through the HDR UK Phenotype Library.
This list has been expanded using the Exeter Diabetes Group CPRD codelist:
https://github.com/Exeter-Diabetes/CPRD-Codelists/blob/main/Medcodes/Comorbidities/exeter_medcodelist_copd.txt
The final list includes only COPD diagnosis codes:

Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease
Chronic obstructive airway disease (COAD, COLD)
Chronic bronchitis (chronic/obstructive forms only)
Emphysema (all COPD‑related subtypes)
Chronic airflow obstruction/limitation
COPD severity codes
COPD with acute lower respiratory infection (diagnosis with complication)

The list excludes:

History-of codes
Monitoring/review codes
Exacerbation-only codes
Asthma or ACOS
Non‑obstructive bronchitis
Structural emphysema (blebs, bullae)
Interstitial emphysema and air‑leak syndromes

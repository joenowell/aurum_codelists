This myocardial infarction codelist was created using SNOMED CT concepts.
The initial codelist was derived from the NHS England Primary Care Domain Reference Set for myocardial infarction, obtained via OpenCodelists:
Source: https://www.opencodelists.org/codelist/nhsd-primary-care-domain-refsets/mi_cod/20250912
The reference set was developed to identify myocardial infarction diagnoses recorded in UK primary care electronic health records and is suitable for use with SNOMED‑native systems such as CPRD Aurum.
Following extraction, the codelist was reviewed and refined for use as the myocardial infarction component of a standard three‑point MACE outcome (myocardial infarction, stroke, or cardiovascular death) in a target‑trial emulation context. The following types of codes were excluded:

Post‑myocardial infarction complications (e.g. pericarditis, ventricular rupture, thrombus)
Recovery‑phase or follow‑up diagnoses
Treatment or procedure‑related records
Non‑specific or mixed ischaemic diagnoses
Microinfarction or retrospectively identified infarction concepts

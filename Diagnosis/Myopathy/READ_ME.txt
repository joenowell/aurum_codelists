This codelist was created to identify pre‑existing myopathy for use as a baseline exclusion criterion (e.g. prior to statin initiation) in UK primary care electronic health records.
The codelist was developed using SNOMED CT codes recorded in CPRD Aurum. Candidate codes were identified by searching for diagnosis‑level concepts using the following key search terms:

*myopathy*
*myositis*
*rhabdomyolysis*

Search results were reviewed refined to retain only codes representing true skeletal muscle disease relevant to statin safety. Codes representing muscle pain alone, localised or infective muscle conditions, or non‑muscle disease were excluded.
For use in CPRD Aurum, the final codelist was restricted to diagnosis‑level records only (EmisCodeCategoryId = 32).
